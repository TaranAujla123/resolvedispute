Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not.

Short, because there is not much to say. If anything here is unclear, ask me directly.

What you send me

If you complete an inquiry, the details go to me by email and nowhere else. I do not sell, rent or share them. I use them to respond to you and, if the matter proceeds, to run it. Nothing is sent to the other party without your say-so, and you can ask me to delete your information at any time.

What the site measures

These pages carry the Meta advertising pixel, which tells me whether the advertising that brought you here is working. It records that a visit and, if you send one, an inquiry happened. It does not receive the contents of your inquiry, and it is not on the private tools linked from this site.

If you would rather not be measured, your browser's tracking protection or an ad blocker will stop it, and nothing on this site stops working as a result.

Mediation itself

What is said in a mediation is treated as private and without prejudice. That protection is strong but it is not absolute, and there are recognised exceptions. I will go through what applies to your matter before we start rather than promise more than I can deliver.
