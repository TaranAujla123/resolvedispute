Renovation · Ontario

The renovation went wrong, and now nobody will move.

The work is unfinished or unacceptable, money has been paid, more is being demanded, and the trust is gone. These are among the most expensive disputes to litigate relative to the amount actually at stake.

Why these go so badly

A renovation dispute has a particular shape. The amount in dispute is often $40,000 to $300,000 — big enough to matter enormously to a homeowner, and small enough that litigating it can consume most of it.

  • Neither side has an insurer funding the fight, unlike most litigation.
  • The evidence is a half-finished house, and it deteriorates while you argue.
  • The homeowner cannot easily bring anyone else in to finish while a dispute is live.
  • The contractor has cash-flow pressure and often a lien deadline.
  • Both sides believe, genuinely, that they are the one being taken advantage of.

It is the classic case where both parties end up worse off than almost any settlement they could have reached in month one.

The two roads

Litigated

Expert reports on both sides about the quality of the work, which cost real money before anyone has argued anything.

A year or more, with the house in the state it is in now.

Legal costs that can approach or exceed the amount in dispute.

And a judgment against a contractor who may not be collectable.

Mediated

A single jointly instructed assessment of what is actually done and what it would cost to finish, instead of two competing ones.

The money split against that number rather than against two opinions.

Frequently a completion arrangement — the work finished on defined terms with a holdback, which is what the homeowner actually wants.

Weeks. Fixed fee, split.

One thing to check today

Before anything else

If a construction lien may be involved, there are deadlines, and they are short. That is a question for a lawyer and it is worth asking this week rather than next.

Mediating does not remove those deadlines. Ask your own counsel what applies to you, and then decide how you want to resolve the substance.
